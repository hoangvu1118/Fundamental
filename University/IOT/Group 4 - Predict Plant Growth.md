## Agriculture Timeline
9000 BC : With bare hands, no innovation , no technology, just human
4500 BC: Tools started to appear, but yet still no strategy/ plans, that's a gamble with nature
1500s AD : Innovations started to appear, people know when to start and end their _harvest season_
2000s : Digital farming -> Technology started to optimize farmer's tasks
## Plants requirements
1. Water
2. Sunlight
3. CO2 levels
--> Greenhouse: control the environment in which plants grow, especially **Temperature**

>[!Notion] 
>Each plant will have their own **OPTIMUM** temperature to grow, and also the base and the maximum temperature that it can endure during extreme weather

--> To let the plant grow --> Feeds it with sufficient amount of temperature, water, fertilizer

## GDD : Growing Degree Days
It only **applicable** when *water, nutrients and CO2 levels* are adequate
![[Pasted image 20250327230117.png]]
Tmax : max temperature in a day
Tmin : min temperature in a day
Tbase : a required temperature for each specific plant to grow

Example : A corn needs 800 to 2700 GDD to completely mature
Say its base temperature is 10 degree
The highest average temperature recorded in Vietnam was 40 and lowest was 23 
so simple math --> we ends up with GDD for 1 day
